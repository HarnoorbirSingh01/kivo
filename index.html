Kivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Kivo ("we", "our", "us") is an education app for JEE/NEET students, published by Infino. This Privacy Policy explains what information Kivo collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Kivo, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Kivo collects the following information to operate the app:

  • Account information: When you sign in (via Firebase Authentication), we collect your name, email address, and a unique user ID. If you sign in with Google, basic profile information provided by Google is collected.
  • User-generated content: Notes, doubts/questions, photos, and answers you upload or post are stored using Supabase storage, so that they can be displayed to you and, where relevant, shared with the Kivo community.
  • Usage and progress data: Study streaks, XP, focus timer sessions, and leaderboard activity are stored to power gamification features.
  • Subscription and billing information: If you purchase a premium subscription, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Kivo does not collect or store your payment card details — Google manages this data under its own privacy policy.

Kivo does not currently use analytics or crash-reporting tools, so your usage is not tracked for advertising or analytics purposes beyond what is described above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in securely
  • To store and display your notes, doubts, and community posts
  • To operate gamification features such as streaks, XP, and leaderboards
  • To moderate community content for safety (see Section 3)
  • To manage premium subscriptions and unlock features you've paid for
  • To respond to support requests

3. Content Moderation

To keep the community safe, content posted to Kivo (such as doubts, answers, and photos) is automatically screened using automated moderation tools, including text and image classification. This process is automated and is used only to detect content that may violate our community guidelines.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that help us run Kivo:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and account data storage
  • Supabase: storage of notes, doubts, photos, and related content
  • Google Play Billing: processing of subscription payments

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

Kivo may be used by students under the age of 13. We are committed to protecting the privacy of younger users:

  • We collect only the information necessary for the app to function (account sign-in, study content, and progress data), as described above.
  • We do not knowingly use children's data for advertising, and Kivo does not display third-party ads.
  • Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information and would like it reviewed, updated, or deleted can contact us using the details in Section 8.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and content data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (Section 8); we will remove your data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including secure authentication, access-controlled storage policies, and encrypted connections between the app and our service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
