Delete Your Kivo Account or Data

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Kivo, published by Infino, gives you full control over your account and personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of your account, or of specific data, without deleting your entire account.

Option 1: Delete Your Entire Account

To permanently delete your Kivo account and all associated data:

  1. Send an email to harnoorbir001@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Kivo account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Account".
  3. Include your registered email address and, if known, your account name in the body of the email.
Your account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days of a verified request.

Option 2: Delete Specific Data (Keep Your Account)

If you'd like certain data removed without deleting your account entirely:

  1. Send an email to harnoorbir001@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Data".
  3. Specify what you'd like removed (e.g. specific notes, doubts/answers, uploaded photos, or progress/streak history).
Requested data will be deleted within 30 days of a verified request. Your account and login will remain active unless you separately request full account deletion.

What Gets Deleted

  • Account information: your name, email address, and authentication details (deleted on full account deletion)
  • User-generated content: notes, doubts, answers, and photos you've uploaded
  • Progress data: streaks, XP, and focus timer history
  • Subscription status: any active premium subscription will be unlinked from your account; note that cancelling billing itself must be done through Google Play, as Kivo does not process payments directly

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited data for a short period after a deletion request where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, records needed to resolve a billing dispute). Any such data is retained only as long as necessary and is not used for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to harnoorbir001@gmail.com.
